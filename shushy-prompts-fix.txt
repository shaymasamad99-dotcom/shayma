شوشي — صور التمارين يلي فيديوهاتها لازم تنعاد (٢١ تمرين)
لكل تمرين صورتين: ١ = البداية، ٢ = النهاية.
مهم: نفس الكاميرا ونفس المكان ونفس الحجم بالصورتين. الإجرين والمات والجهاز ما بيتحرّكوا إلا الشي يلي بيتحرّك بالتمرين.
ابدئي كل برومبت بهيدا الوصف:

STYLE: Cute 3D cartoon girl "Shushy": brown hair in a high bun, pink sports bra, purple leggings, white sneakers with pink details, on a purple mat, plain light blue background, full body visible, centered, square image, clean studio lighting.

────────────────────────────
1) db-hipthrust — هيب ثرست بالدمبل  (من الجنب)
1: STYLE + side view. Upper back (shoulder blades) resting on the edge of a flat bench, hips low near the floor, knees bent, feet flat on the mat shoulder-width apart, ONE dumbbell held horizontally across the hip crease with both hands.
2: STYLE + same camera. Hips driven up so shoulders, hips and knees form one straight line, shins vertical and knees at a 90-degree angle, chin tucked, dumbbell still across the hips, glutes squeezed. Feet and bench in exactly the same place.

2) m-hipthrust — هيب ثرست عالسميث  (من الجنب)
1: STYLE + side view. Smith machine; upper back on a bench, barbell with a pad across the hip crease, hips low, knees bent, feet flat.
2: STYLE + same camera. Hips up, torso parallel to the floor, shins VERTICAL, knees at exactly 90 degrees, straight line shoulders–hips–knees, chin tucked. Feet do not move.

3) m-kickback — ركلة خلفية بالكيبل  (من الجنب)
1: STYLE + side view. Low cable pulley with ankle strap on one ankle, hands holding the machine frame, torso hinged slightly forward (about 30 degrees), core braced, working leg just behind the standing leg, standing knee slightly bent.
2: STYLE + same camera. Working leg extended straight back and slightly up by the glute only, hips square, lower back NOT arched, torso angle unchanged. Standing foot does not move.

4) db-sumo — سكوات سومو بالدمبل  (من الجنب!)
1: STYLE + SIDE view. Wide stance, toes turned out ~45°, standing tall, one dumbbell held vertically by the top end with both hands, arms straight between the legs.
2: STYLE + same side camera. Squatting down: hips pushed BACK, chest up with a neutral spine leaning slightly forward, knees tracking out over the toes, thighs about parallel to the floor, dumbbell hanging straight down between the legs. Feet flat, same place.

5) db-curtsy — طعنة كيرتسي  (من قدّام)
1: STYLE + front view. Standing tall, feet hip-width, a dumbbell in each hand at the sides.
2: STYLE + same camera. Right leg stepped BACK and diagonally behind the left leg (like a curtsy), both knees bent, left (front) knee over the left foot and pointing forward, hips square to the camera, torso upright, back knee hovering just above the mat. Left foot does not move.

6) band-walk — مشي جانبي بالمطاط  (من قدّام، ٣ صور إذا بتقدري)
1: STYLE + front view. Mini band around the legs just above the knees, half-squat athletic stance, feet hip-width, hands together at the chest.
2: STYLE + same camera. Same half squat, right foot stepped out sideways so feet are wide apart, band stretched, toes forward.
3: STYLE + same camera. Left foot brought in, back to hip-width, but the whole body shifted to the right of image 1 (she is walking sideways).

7) m-cableabd — فتح الإجر بالكيبل واقفة  (من قدّام)
1: STYLE + front view. Standing sideways to a low cable pulley, cable strap on the OUTSIDE ankle (the leg farther from the machine), the near hand holding the machine, torso upright, feet together.
2: STYLE + same camera. The strapped (outside) leg lifted straight out to the side AWAY from the machine to about 30–40°, toes pointing forward, torso upright with no leaning. Standing foot does not move.

8) legraise — رفع رجلين نايمة  (من الجنب)
1: STYLE + side view. Lying on her back on the mat, hands flat under the lower back/glutes, lower back pressed into the mat, legs straight together and held just above the floor.
2: STYLE + same camera. Straight legs raised to 90 degrees (vertical), lower back still flat on the mat, head relaxed on the mat.

9) m-cablecrunch — كرنش بالكيبل  (من الجنب)
1: STYLE + side view. Kneeling facing a high cable pulley, holding a rope attachment beside her head, hips high above the knees, torso upright-ish, spine long.
2: STYLE + same camera. Crunching down by curling the spine (ribs toward pelvis), elbows moving toward the thighs, hips stay in the SAME place (not sitting back). Knees do not move.

10) bicycle — كرنش البسكليت  (من الجنب مايل شوي)
1: STYLE + three-quarter side view. Lying on her back, hands lightly behind the head, shoulders lifted off the mat, right knee pulled toward the chest, left leg extended straight a few cm above the mat, left elbow rotating toward the right knee.
2: STYLE + same camera. Mirror image: left knee in, right leg extended, right elbow rotating toward the left knee. Hips stay in the same place on the mat.

11) rev-crunch — كرنش عكسي  (من الجنب)
1: STYLE + side view. Lying on her back, arms flat by the sides, knees bent at 90° with thighs vertical, feet in the air.
2: STYLE + same camera. Pelvis curled up so the hips lift slightly off the mat and the knees come toward the chest, knees still bent, arms flat. Head stays down.

12) db-woodchop — قطع الحطب بالدمبل  (من قدّام)
1: STYLE + front view. Feet wider than shoulders, holding ONE dumbbell with both hands high above the RIGHT shoulder, arms extended, torso rotated to the right.
2: STYLE + same camera. Dumbbell brought diagonally down across the body to outside the LEFT knee, knees bent, torso rotated to the left, arms straight. Feet stay in place (back foot can pivot).

13) m-abcrunch — جهاز البطن  (من الجنب)
1: STYLE + side view. Seated in an ab crunch machine, chest pad against the chest (or handles by the shoulders), feet hooked under the foot pads, torso upright.
2: STYLE + same camera. Torso curled forward by flexing the abs (ribs toward hips), pads moved down, hips and feet in the same place.

14) m-facepull-seat — فيس بول قاعدة  (من الجنب)
1: STYLE + side view. Sitting on the floor facing a cable machine, legs straight or slightly bent, rope attached HIGH on the pulley (above head height), arms extended up and forward toward the pulley, cable going UP.
2: STYLE + same camera. Rope pulled toward the FACE: hands beside the ears, elbows high and flared out at shoulder height, shoulder blades squeezed, torso upright. The cable still goes UP to the high pulley.

15) m-preacher — جهاز باي بريتشر  (من الجنب) — نفس الجهاز بالصورتين!
1: STYLE + side view. Seated at a preacher curl machine, upper arms flat on the angled pad, arms almost straight, holding the handles.
2: STYLE + same camera, SAME machine. Elbows curled so the handles come up near the shoulders, upper arms still on the pad, back straight.

16) bench-dips — غطس عالبنش  (من الجنب)
1: STYLE + side view. Hands on the edge of a flat bench behind her, fingers forward, arms straight, legs extended forward with heels on the mat, hips just in front of the bench.
2: STYLE + same camera. Elbows bent to about 90° pointing BACK, hips lowered straight down close to the bench, shoulders down away from the ears, chest up. Hands and heels do not move.

17) db-reardelt — رفرفة خلفية بالدمبل  (من قدّام مايل)
1: STYLE + three-quarter front view. Standing, hinged forward at the hips so the torso is nearly parallel to the floor, flat back, soft knees, arms hanging down with a dumbbell in each hand, palms facing each other.
2: STYLE + same camera. Arms raised out to the sides to shoulder height with slightly bent elbows, shoulder blades squeezed, torso still hinged and still.

18) m-pushdown — دفع الكيبل للتراي  (من الجنب، واقفة)
1: STYLE + side view. STANDING facing a high cable pulley, slight forward lean, elbows pinned at her sides, forearms up near the chest holding a straight bar/rope.
2: STYLE + same camera. Arms fully straightened down, elbows still pinned at the sides (upper arms do not move), wrists straight.

19) m-captain — كرسي القبطان رفع الركب  (من الجنب)
1: STYLE + side view. Back against the pad of a captain's chair, forearms on the arm pads, legs hanging straight down.
2: STYLE + same camera. Knees raised up toward the chest (thighs above parallel), back stays on the pad, no swinging.

20) m-abduction — جهاز فتح الرجلين  (من قدّام)
1: STYLE + front view. Seated in a hip abduction machine, back on the pad, pads on the OUTSIDE of the knees, knees together.
2: STYLE + same camera. Knees pushed wide APART against the pads (clearly open), back on the pad, feet on the foot rests.

21) db-bench — ضغط صدر بالدمبل  (من الجنب)
1: STYLE + side view. Lying on a flat bench, feet flat on the floor, dumbbells lowered to the MIDDLE of the chest, held ACROSS the body (seen end-on from the side, the dumbbell is a circle, not a long bar), elbows about 45° from the body.
2: STYLE + same camera. Arms pressed straight up above the mid-chest, dumbbells still across (end-on), shoulder blades on the bench.
